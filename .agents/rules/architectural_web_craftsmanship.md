# Architectural Web Craftsmanship Rule (Awwwards Level Invariants)

## 1. Structural Typography & Hierarchy
- Headings MUST use architectural font styles with tracked letter spacing (`letter-spacing: -0.02em` to `0.1em`).
- Combine high-contrast bold dark lead-ins (`#141414`) with translucent charcoal body flows (`#777777`) for dynamic reading rhythm.

## 2. Frameless Architectural Grids & Hairline Dividers
- Prefer open layouts separated by delicate hairline dividers (`1px solid rgba(20,20,20,0.12)`) over heavy solid boxes or cards.
- Maintain generous negative space (vertical padding 4rem-6rem) to let photography and architectural text breathe naturally.

## 3. Dynamic Interactive Work Process & Portfolios
- Present multi-step workflows as frameless architectural timelines with subtle left/top hairline highlights on active items.
- Synchronize list hover/active states with large-format photography containers on the side.

## 4. Organic Color Palette & Material Contrast
- Use authentic plaster tones (`#F4F3EE`), pitch charcoal (`#141414`), and warm off-whites (`#FFFFFF`). Never introduce raw browser colors or solid dark blocks on light themes.
